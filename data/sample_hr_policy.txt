Company Leave Policy

Employees get 18 casual leaves and 12 sick leaves every year.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks. Paternity leave is 7 days.
Unused casual leaves can be carried forward up to 5 days into the next year.
To apply for leave, employees must submit a request in the HR portal at least 2 days in advance.
Emergency leave can be approved by the manager on the same day.
