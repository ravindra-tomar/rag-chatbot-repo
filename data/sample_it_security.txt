IT Security Policy

All employees must use strong passwords with at least 12 characters.
Password sharing is strictly prohibited.
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for email and HR portal login.
Official data must not be stored on personal Google Drive or USB drives.
Suspicious emails should be reported to it-security@company.com immediately.
Lost or stolen laptop must be reported to IT within 1 hour.
Software installation requires IT admin approval.
