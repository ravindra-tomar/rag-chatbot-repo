Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Core overlapping hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM India time for meetings.
A stable internet connection of at least 40 Mbps is required.
Company laptop must be used for official work; personal devices are not allowed for accessing internal systems.
WFH status must be updated in the attendance portal every morning.
Security VPN must remain connected during work hours.
